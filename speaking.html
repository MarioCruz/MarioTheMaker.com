Booking keynotes, panels, and residencies for 2026–2027.

I speak about maker culture, robotics, and what happens when you put poetry, AI, and a soldering iron in the same room. I've shared work at DockerCon, Maker Faire, NSF programs, and museum stages — and I've been doing this for over a decade.

Maker Culture & the Future of Work

Why the maker movement is the most underrated workforce-development engine of the next decade. Drawing on Maker Faire Miami, Maker Cities (NSF), and a decade of teaching adults and kids to build.

Poetry × Technology

Ten years of building Poetry Robots with O, Miami. Why language belongs on hardware, and how unexpected collisions between art and engineering produce the most memorable public installations.

Restoring the Past with AI

What it takes to give a 1984 Tomy Omnibot computer vision and autonomous behavior. A practical talk on vintage hardware, modern silicon, and shipping live demos that don't fall over.
