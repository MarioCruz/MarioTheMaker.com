About Mario The Maker

Technologist · Speaker · Producer · Builder

I'm Mario Cruz, a Miami-based technologist, entrepreneur, inventor, and musician. As a kid, I would take things apart and mostly put them back together. Then I started fixing musical instruments and building computers, and I was hooked. I studied music at Miami Dade College, co-founded Choose Digital in 2011 (acquired by Viggle Inc. in 2014), and have been CTO at Watsco Ventures since 2015. Today I work out of Moonlighter FabLab in Miami, building robots, IoT devices, and interactive installations.

Currently

  • Restoring a 1984 Tomy Omnibot with Ring-O, a 3D-printed AI sidekick built around a Raspberry Pi 5 and IMX500 AI camera, for Maker Faire Miami 2026.
  • Producing Maker Faire Miami 2026 with the Moonlighter team.

Speaking & Community

I've spoken at DockerCon, Jenkins User Conference, DevOps Days, Sime Miami, and Google Startup Grind. I host MeetUps for Docker, DevOps, and Raspberry Pi open-source electronics, and I judge the South Florida Regional FIRST Robotics Competition.

Featured Work

My projects have been featured at Miami Maker Faire, Maker Faire Orlando, Maker Faire Bay Area, the O, Miami Poetry Festival, Frost Science Museum, Fairchild Tropical Botanic Garden, and DockerCon. Recent builds include the Banana Poem Phone (a landline phone that plays poems when dialed, made for the 2026 O, Miami Poetry Festival) and Ring-O (a 3D-printed AI robotic sidekick for a restored 1984 Tomy Omnibot).
