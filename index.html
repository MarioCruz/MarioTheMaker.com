Mario The Maker

Hacker, Maker, Inventor, and Musician from Miami. Working out of Moonlighter Makerspace. Hot Sauce Maker.

I'm Mario Cruz. A Miami-based maker, technologist, and inventor. I build poetry robots, restore vintage toys with modern AI, and run Maker Faire Miami as its Executive Producer. This site documents the projects I've built at Moonlighter FabLab, from interactive installations for the O, Miami Poetry Festival to open-source hardware on GitHub. Follow along on Instagram.
